# Hyeto brand policy

The PolyForm Noncommercial License covers copyright permissions for the software. It does not grant trademark, endorsement or product-naming rights.

## Upstream marks

Hyeto is a fork. It uses a different name and identity from the upstream project, as that project's brand policy requires. “Rainform / 数据成雨” and afterimage's icon and official social artwork remain the upstream project's identity, and may be used only to describe the origin of the software, for example:

> Includes Rainform / 数据成雨 by afterimage — https://rainform.pages.dev/

You may not:

- name another product, preset or service “Rainform” or “数据成雨”;
- use the Rainform icon or official social artwork as another product's icon;
- imply that Hyeto, or any fork or integration of it, is an official or endorsed Rainform release;
- remove the required copyright and source notice.

## Hyeto's own name

The same limits apply to “Hyeto”. Use it only to describe the origin of this fork, and do not present a further fork or integration as an official Hyeto release. Forks of Hyeto should use a clearly different name and visual identity, and must carry both the upstream Required Notice and their own attribution.
